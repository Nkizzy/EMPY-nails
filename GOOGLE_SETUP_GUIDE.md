# Google Setup Guide for EMPY Nails

## 🎯 **Essential Google Services for Local Business SEO**

### 1. **Google Business Profile (Formerly Google My Business)**
**Priority: CRITICAL**

#### Setup Steps:
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Manage now" or "Add your business to Google"
4. Enter business details:
   - **Business name**: EMPY Nails
   - **Category**: Nail Salon
   - **Address**: 2298 Monroe Ave, Rochester, NY 14618
   - **Phone**: (585) 270-5940
   - **Website**: https://empynails.com
   - **Hours**: 
     - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
     - Monday-Saturday: 9:30 AM - 7:30 PM

#### Verification:
- Choose "Postcard by mail" verification
- Wait 1-2 weeks for postcard
- Enter verification code when received

#### Optimization Tips:
- Add high-quality photos of your salon, work, and team
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)
- Post regular updates about services, promotions, events
- Use relevant keywords in posts and descriptions

### 2. **Google Search Console**
**Priority: HIGH**

#### Setup Steps:
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add your property: `https://empynails.com`
3. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **HTML file**: Upload `google1234567890abcdef.html` to your server
   - **HTML tag**: Add meta tag to `<head>` section
   - **DNS record**: Add TXT record to your domain

#### What to Monitor:
- **Coverage**: Check for crawl errors
- **Performance**: Monitor search queries and clicks
- **Mobile Usability**: Ensure mobile-friendly design
- **Core Web Vitals**: Page speed and user experience metrics

### 3. **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**
**Priority: HIGH**

#### Setup Steps:
1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Create new property for EMPY Nails
3. Get tracking code and add to your website
4. Set up goals for:
   - Phone calls
   - Contact form submissions
   - Service page visits
   - Gallery interactions

#### Key Metrics to Track:
- **Traffic sources**: Where visitors come from
- **User behavior**: Which pages they visit
- **Conversions**: Phone calls, form submissions
- **Mobile vs Desktop**: Device usage patterns

### 4. **Google Ads (Optional but Recommended)**
**Priority: MEDIUM**

#### Local Service Ads:
- Target local searches like "nail salon near me"
- Focus on Rochester, NY area
- Use keywords: nail salon, gel nails, pedicure, manicure
- Set budget: Start with $10-20/day

#### Search Ads:
- Target specific services: "gel nail extensions Rochester"
- Use location targeting for Monroe Ave area
- Include phone number in ads for direct calls
